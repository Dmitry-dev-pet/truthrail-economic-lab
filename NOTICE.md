# Attribution and limitations

This repository wraps an independent mathematical reproduction of the 2026 Anthropic Institute working paper *Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI* (Korinek, Jones, Sacher, Cotter, McCrory).

Independent implementation: Fernando Hoces de la Guardia, MIT licensed:
https://github.com/fhoces/opa-ai-macro-econ-scenarios

The Docker image downloads the upstream implementation at **v1.0**, commit
d4703b6ae0d19dca08fbde11a25c218fa4c7b7a6, and preserves its LICENSE in /opt/opa-model.

The Anthropic Institute did not publish this code. The simulation is a **conditional US macroeconomic scenario**, not a factual forecast of jobs, GDP, or the economy of Portugal.
